## 🧭 What we do

WeaverAI designs and builds **AI-native software** — the infrastructure, agents, and
tooling that make intelligent products dependable at scale. We care about clean
architecture, strong reliability, and getting real value into users' hands.

We keep most of the details behind the curtain, but here's the high-level picture.

## 🧩 What we're building

| Area | What it's about |
| --- | --- |
| **Platform** | Backend infrastructure and services that power our AI products. |
| **Agents** | Autonomous and assistive agents that do useful, real-world work. |
| **Developer tooling** | Utilities and libraries that make building with AI faster and safer. |
| **Experiments** | Early-stage research and prototypes we're not quite ready to talk about. |

> Individual project details are intentionally kept light here. Reach out if you'd
> like to learn more.

## 🛠️ How we build

- **Reliable by default** — thoughtful architecture, observability, and graceful failure.
- **Pragmatic** — the simplest thing that solves the real problem.
- **Open where it helps** — we share tools and ideas that benefit the wider community.
